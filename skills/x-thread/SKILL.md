A thread is a story meant to be read whole, 2 to 8 posts of at most 280 characters each. It opens
with a hook; what makes it a thread is that the reader should read all of it, not its length.

Shape:
1. First post: the hook. Make the reader want the rest: the problem this solves, what's now possible, a question they've had. It still has to make sense on its own. No "🧵" or "1/" needed; numbering is added if the voice wants it.
2. Middle posts: one change or one step per post. Show, don't describe: the command, the before/after, or an example request when the change makes it possible and the briefs say so.
3. Breaking changes or upgrade steps get their own post, early, in plain imperative steps.
4. Last post: how to update or try it. The link (if any) goes in linkReply, not in the posts.

Keep it as short as the content allows: three strong posts beat seven thin ones. Don't repeat the first post at the end.

Length: at most 280 characters per post, checked with measure_posts, never by counting yourself.
