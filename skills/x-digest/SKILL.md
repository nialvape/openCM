name: x-digest
description: How to write a digest that groups several small changes (fixes, minor improvements) into 1 to 4 posts.
---
A digest bundles small changes that wouldn't deserve a post alone.

Shape:
1. First post: a one-line framing ("Mejoras de la semana en …" / "Small fixes in …") plus the most useful item.
2. Remaining items as short lines, grouped by who they help. Several items can share a post; one line each, symptom-first for fixes ("el login de FRT ya no falla cuando…").
3. Split into more posts only when one post can't hold the lines within 280 characters.

Leave out anything internal. If after removing internal items fewer than two remain, say so plainly in a single post.

Length: at most 280 characters per post, checked with measure_posts, never by counting yourself.
